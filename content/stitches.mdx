The Stitches Doll Project began in 1999 as a way for women affected by HIV/AIDS to tell their stories through handmade dolls. The collection grew to include experiences of violence, bleeding disorders, and other challenges. I'm rebuilding the website for the project's founder, Kathy: the design, the development, and the migration from the old site.

## rebuilding the archive

The original site was a set of static HTML pages. I wrote a Python pipeline to extract the doll records into structured content with schema validation. Matching photographs to the right dolls and separating shared entries took review rather than parsing, so I built local tools for those decisions.

## designing for reading

The stories are the reason to visit. The gallery filters the collection and shows stories in rows, with longer stories expanding in place. Each doll also has its own page. The visual direction is warm and handmade, and the dolls' photographs do most of the work.

## implementation

Astro builds the site from typed content collections. Pages CMS gives Kathy an editing interface, and Cloudflare Pages hosts it. Redirects from the old pages are mapped for the move. The rebuild is in development; the public site hasn't been replaced yet.
