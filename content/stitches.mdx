The Stitches Doll Project began in 1999 as a way for women affected by HIV/AIDS to tell their stories through handmade dolls. The collection grew to include experiences of violence, bleeding disorders, and other challenges. I redesigned and rebuilt the project's website for its founder, Kathy.

## The redesign

The photographs of the dolls do most of the work. The homepage introduces the project, then leads into stories from the collection, the three programs, Kathy's history with Stitches, and ways to get involved.

<Figure src="/projects/stitches/images/stitches-home.webp" alt="The redesigned Stitches homepage with its introduction beside a photograph of three handmade dolls" width={1440} height={792} caption="the homepage [october 2026 development version]" />

The globe connects places in the archive to the women who made the dolls. Choosing a country turns the globe and brings up stories from that country.

<Figure src="/projects/stitches/images/stitches-reach.webp" alt="The interactive globe beside Creation's doll and story, with country choices below" width={1440} height={960} caption="places and the stories they hold" />

## Collection

The stories are the reason to visit, so the site is organized around the collection. I reworked the sitemap so events are groupings within the gallery instead of pages of their own. The gallery filters the collection and lists stories in rows, longer stories expand in place, and every doll has its own page.

## Implementation

Astro builds the site from typed content collections, with Cloudflare Pages for hosting. These captures show the October 2026 development version; final client assets and the editing setup are still being worked out.
