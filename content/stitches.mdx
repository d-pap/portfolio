The Stitches Doll Project hired me to design and build their new website. It's an HIV/AIDS nonprofit that helps people tell their stories through handmade dolls.

I worked with its founder, Kathy, on the site structure and visual design, then built the site and set up its hosting.

## The website

The website introduces the nonprofit and its programs, tells the story of how it started, and gives people ways to get involved. Visitors can explore the dolls, find resources, and learn how to share a story, host an event, or support the work.

<Figure src="/projects/stitches/images/stitches-home.webp" alt="The redesigned Stitches homepage with its introduction beside a photograph of three handmade dolls" width={1440} height={792} caption="The homepage" />

I built an interactive globe that connects places to the people who made the dolls. Choosing a country turns the globe and brings up stories from that country.

<Figure src="/projects/stitches/images/stitches-reach.webp" alt="The interactive globe beside Creation's doll and story, with country choices below" width={1440} height={960} caption="Places and the stories they hold" />

## Collection

The collection lets visitors browse the dolls and read the stories behind them. I reworked its navigation so events are groupings within the gallery instead of pages of their own. The gallery has filters and lists stories in rows, longer stories expand in place, and every doll has its own page.

## Implementation

I built the site with Astro and TypeScript, using typed content collections for the dolls and their stories and Cloudflare Pages for hosting.
