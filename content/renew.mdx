RENEW Scleroderma is a Michigan Medicine study app that helps people with scleroderma manage fatigue. Participants set wellness goals, track how they're feeling, work through lessons, and talk with [Sprout](/work/sprout), an AI health coach.

I took over development of the app and now handle its design, React Native frontend, backend, and releases.

## Redesign

I redesigned the app around the things participants return to each day: checking in, working on goals, and finding a lesson. The new Home screen opens with a daily mood check-in and a weekly trend. I also rebuilt the chat, profile, and login screens.

## Backend

I maintain a PHP and MySQL API on Michigan Medicine servers, with schema changes managed as Knex migrations. Sign-in uses one-time email codes, and endpoints share authentication and input validation.

The backend also connects RENEW to Sprout. A participant's study group determines which coaching features are available and when they can use the chat. Each message includes their active goals, schedules, recent check-offs, and enrolled modules, giving the coach context for the conversation. Sprout identifies participants through a separate ID assigned by the backend.

On the app side, TanStack Query caches server data and the tab screens load at startup, so switching tabs doesn't wait on the network. Lesson images and audio come from the RENEW WordPress site through a media proxy.

## Upgrade

Alongside the redesign, I brought the app through three Expo upgrades and updated Android compatibility for Google Play. I scripted production deployments and manage builds, testing, and releases through TestFlight and the app stores.
