RENEW helps people living with scleroderma set wellness goals, track how they're feeling, and work through lessons on fatigue self-management. It includes [Sprout](/work/rag-api), the AI health coach I work on. I took over the app in September 2025: the React Native app, its PHP backend, the research admin portal, and releases.

## the redesign

I upgraded the React Native and Expo codebase and replaced one-off styling with shared components and typed design tokens. Spacing, type, color, and motion now come from one system instead of per-screen values.

I'm redesigning the daily check-in, goals, learning modules, and profile on that system.

## the research tools

The study team manages participants as well as supporting them. I built an admin-portal workflow for importing assessment data, reviewing generated clinical summaries, and saving them for the health coach to use. The researcher maps each upload to participants explicitly, so the mapping can be reviewed before a summary is saved.

## implementation

The app uses React Native, Expo, TypeScript, TanStack Query, and Zustand. The backend is PHP and MySQL on Michigan Medicine servers. I replaced the magic-link login with email codes and consolidated authentication checks across the API.

Changes move through development and TestFlight. The redesign is ongoing, and the public release doesn't include every screen shown here.
