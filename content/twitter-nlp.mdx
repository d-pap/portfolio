## overview

I analyzed roughly 61,000 tweets about CS:GO and other games, looking at player sentiment and recurring complaints.

I framed the project as a hypothetical marketing brief for Valve: what could the company learn from people talking about its games and competitors?

## the analysis

I compared sentiment across games and looked more closely at the topics behind those labels. Cheating complaints, streaming, and cosmetic items stood out as subjects worth investigating.

I used those observations to suggest marketing experiments. They were proposals from an analysis, not campaigns I ran or measured.

## the model

I compared logistic regression, gradient boosting, and a multilayer perceptron. The MLP had the highest accuracy in the original analysis, at 94.7%.

That score describes the classification experiment. It doesn't tell us whether an ad would work, and applying the model to a different audience or time period would require more evaluation.

The [notebook](https://github.com/d-pap/NLP-sentiment-analysis) contains the preprocessing, model comparisons, and exploratory analysis.
