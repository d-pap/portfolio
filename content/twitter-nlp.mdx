I analyzed about 61,000 tweets about CS:GO and other games to compare player sentiment and find recurring complaints. I used Python for the text analysis and trained sentiment classifiers with scikit-learn and TensorFlow.

## What players talked about

Cheating, streaming, and cosmetic items stood out as topics worth investigating.

## Classifying sentiment

A multilayer perceptron classified sentiment with 94.7% accuracy.
